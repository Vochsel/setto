# Picking the keepers

## What to judge

Judge them as product photography, not as pretty pictures:

- **Is the product readable?** Cropped, obscured, or turned away means the shot
  fails at its only job, however nice it looks.
- **Does the product match its reference?** Wrong colour, wrong neckline,
  invented details — this is the most common failure and the easiest to miss if
  you're looking at the composition instead of the garment.
- **Anatomy and artefacts.** Hands, fingers, text on labels, patterns that
  smear. Look before you send.
- **Is it usable where it's going?** A product page wants clean and centred; a
  social post can be looser.

## Marking

Favourite the keepers with `mark_image` as soon as they say they like one —
that's what makes them findable later, and it's the memory that makes next
week's suggestions better. Rate the rest if they give you an opinion worth
recording.

## Reporting back

Say which you'd keep and why, in one or two lines. If several failed the same
way, name the pattern and what you'd change — a different person, a tighter
crop, a different location — rather than listing each failure separately.
